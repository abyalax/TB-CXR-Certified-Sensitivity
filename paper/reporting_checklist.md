# Preliminary STARD-AI Reporting Checklist

This is a draft-completeness audit for the exploratory image-level study, not a submission checklist or a quality score. 
The checklist follows the 40-item STARD-AI statement and supplementary checklist 
([official article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03953-8), 
[official checklist PDF](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41591-025-03953-8/MediaObjects/41591_2025_3953_MOESM3_ESM.pdf)). 
Status refers to `imrad.md` as it stands on 31 October 2026; open items must be resolved before submission.

| Item                                                                                                          | Status         | Current location or required follow-up                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Identify an AI-centered diagnostic accuracy study and report an accuracy measure in the title or abstract. | Partial        | `imrad.md` title and abstract report sensitivity/specificity, but identify this as a methodological audit rather than a diagnostic accuracy validation.               |
| 2. Structured summary of design, methods, results, and conclusions.                                           | Covered        | `abstract.md` and `imrad.md` abstract; synchronize final robustness outputs before release.                                                                           |

| 5. Prospective or retrospective study design.                                                                 | Covered        | `imrad.md` describes a retrospective secondary analysis.                                                                                                              |
